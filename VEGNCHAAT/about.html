Our Story

A fine gastronomic
break

Born from a deep love for authentic Indian street food, Vegn Chaat is where tradition meets creativity. Every dish tells a story — of spices passed down through generations, of streets alive with flavor, of moments that linger long after the last bite.

We welcome you in a remarkable setting that will transport you, for the duration of a meal, into the vibrant heart of India's most beloved culinary culture.
